# AI-generated imagery

All pictures in `src/assets/ai/` were generated on 2026-11-24 with the **image generation tool of Codex CLI 0.172.0**
(OpenAI), then converted to WebP (`scripts/optimize-ai-images.mjs`). They are illustrative only and show no real
people, places or products. The site footer says so.

Documents in the pictures are completely filled with realistic content and use only invented names; no real organisations, emblems or brands appear.

Shared art direction for every prompt: *cinematic, photorealistic, premium editorial look; deep navy-blue shadows with
warm amber and antique-gold highlights; shallow depth of field; subtle film grain; no text, logos, watermarks or visible
faces.*

## Icons

The twelve icons in `src/assets/icons/` come from two sheets generated by Codex (six icons each, on pure black):
clock, telephone, sealed letter, compass-star location pin, house with key, hourglass (sheet A); shield with
keyhole, balance scales, desk calendar, fountain pen nib with signature, certificate with rosette, heart in an open
hand (sheet B). Style: engraved Art Deco monoline in antique gold with fine parallel hairlines and one small
sparkle. `scripts/make-icons.mjs <folder with icons-a.png and icons-b.png>` cuts the sheets into transparent
320 px WebP masks, which CSS colours (`mask-image`), so they work on light and dark sections.

Check the output licence and AI-disclosure rules that apply to your client's use before reusing these images
commercially.
